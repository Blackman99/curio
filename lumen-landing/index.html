    Lumen 2.0 — now rendering on your GPU

    Interfacesthat feel alive.

    Lumen is a motion engine for the web. Shaders, spring physics and scroll choreography — composed like CSS, shipped as a single file. Move your cursor: the chrome follows you.

    Start building See the engine

    (01) Manifesto

    The web was never meant to be *static.* Every pixel can *think,* every scroll can tell a *story,* every hover can feel like touching something real. Lumen is for people who believe interfaces should move the way nature does — with weight, with intent, and with a little bit of *joy.*

    How a frame
    gets made.

    01 / 04
    01

    Compose

    Describe scenes as layers of shape, light and timing. Lumen compiles them into a single GPU pass.

    02

    Choreograph

    Bind any value to scroll, pointer or time. Every transition is interruptible and physically based.

    03

    Simulate

    Springs, orbits and fields run on a fixed timestep, so motion feels identical at 60 Hz and 120 Hz.

    04

    Ship

    One import, tree-shaken to what you use. Degrades gracefully when the GPU says no.
