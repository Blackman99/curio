Snowflake Net Nº 006

Fold paper the way a ReLU-style network folds space. Each fold is one layer; one straight cut is the final linear neuron. Unfold to see the decision boundary it draws.

fold: x ↦ x − 2·min(0, n·x − b)·n  (an |·| unit)
folds (layers)0max pieces 2k1pieces on sheet1cut edges—
drag = fold along a line · C or shift-drag = cut · U / space = unfold · R = new sheet · P = paper
Toy abs-fold network with hand/randomly drawn folds — not a trained model. The folding intuition is from Montúfar, Pascanu, Cho & Bengio, “On the Number of Linear Regions of Deep Neural Networks” (NeurIPS 2014). Pieces/edges are counted on a sample grid. Fully client-side.
